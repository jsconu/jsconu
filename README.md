## Why I'm building Ondatrace

A date moves in one tool. A promise sits in someone's email. A decision gets made in a meeting and never written down. The important consequence often crosses project boundaries.

Ondatrace is designed to give project managers and PMO leaders a shared, evidence-backed view of what changed, what else it affects, and what needs a decision.

**Status:** Private working prototype under active development, with design-partner validation as the next step. The public demo uses fictional initiatives to illustrate the workflow; it is not evidence of customer results.

[Try the demo](https://ondatrace.com/try/) · [Read the design decisions](https://ondatrace.com/decisions/) · [Product thesis](https://ondatrace.com/thesis/)

## How I build

AI coding agents generate a significant amount of Ondatrace's implementation. I own the product definition, domain model, priorities, acceptance criteria, evaluation design, and review of the resulting behavior.

My focus is the judgment around implementation: what the system should do, where human approval belongs, how it can fail, and what evidence would show that it helps.
